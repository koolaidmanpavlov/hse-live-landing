Getting There & Parking

Turning Stone Resort Casino is at 5218 Patrick Road, Verona, New York — about thirty minutes east of Syracuse, right off Thruway Exit 33.

Self parking in the resort garages is free, and complimentary valet is available at the Tower main entrance.

Park in the North Parking garage. It brings you in through the Tree of Peace area, which is the quickest route to our meeting rooms. Other lots will route you toward the Expo Center, which is the wrong end of the property.

Check In & Registration

Check in at the table outside your own room — there is no single desk. Wednesday morning: outside Seneca for the Fiscal Summit and outside Oak for CLASS Certification, from 8:00. Wednesday evening: outside Onondaga for the Celebration. Thursday morning: outside the Mohawk Ballroom, from 8:00. Pick up your badge and materials there.

Digital reader boards outside each room list our session names, locations and times, refreshed each day. Turning Stone staff are all over the property and happy to point you anywhere.

Good to Know

  • Bring photo ID for Thursday evening. The Turquoise Tiger sits inside the gaming floor, so security checks ID at the door — everyone, every age, no exceptions. Leave it in your room and you will be walking back for it.
  • Dress is business casual.
  • Wear comfortable shoes. Turning Stone is a large property and the Turquoise Tiger is a good walk from the meeting rooms.
  • Bring a light layer. September evenings in central New York can be cool.
  • Bring business cards. Some of the best parts of this conference happen between sessions. Make it easy for people to find you afterward.
  • Bring something from your program. A brochure, a pin, a sticker — anything that shows the work you do. It's an easy way to start a conversation, and we love seeing the range of programs in the room.
  • Need us on site? Stop by the Head Start East LIVE registration table — someone from our team is always there.
